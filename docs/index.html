Shortcuts & Gestures

Almost everything is a click with a modifier held. Every gesture below can be switched off individually in Settings.

Marking equipment
Click
Open a piece and read its details. The first click only inspects — it changes nothing.
Click again
On the piece already open: marks it owned. Armor then rises one upgrade level per click up to its maximum.
Ctrl / ⌘ + Click
Own it, or un-own it. This is the way back out if you marked something by mistake.
Alt + Click
Own it at its highest level in one go.
Shift + Click
Put it on the checklist, or take it off.
Moving around the grid or list
← ↑ ↓ → or W A S D
Step through the pieces, showing each one as you land on it. Nothing is marked by moving. Not while you are typing in the search box.
In the detail panel
Click a level row
Armor: set the piece to that level. Clicking the level it is already at un-owns it.
Click a weapon name
In the upgrade tree, jump to the weapon it upgrades from or into.
In crafting routes
Click a row
Show that weapon in the detail panel. Clicking it again does not mark it here.
Click a weapon name
Jump to a base or branch weapon named in a route.
On the checklist
Click a row header
Show or hide that piece's materials. A plain click anywhere else on the checklist deliberately does nothing, so reading a list can't change what you own.
− +
Count a material down or up. Hold either one and it repeats.
Type in a Have box
Set a count outright. It applies when you press Enter or leave the field.
Ctrl Alt Shift + Click
Still work here, so you can tick a piece off as built without leaving the list.
Saving
Ctrl / ⌘ + S
Save to your file. Your collection is also kept in this browser automatically, unless you have turned that off.

About / Attributions

An unofficial, non-commercial fan tool for tracking your Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate equipment collection. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Capcom.

Game content

Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate and all of its names, data tables and icons are © CAPCOM CO., LTD. All rights reserved. Everything here was extracted from a personally owned copy of the game and is shown for reference only; no game files are redistributed.

Where the data comes from

Every stat, recipe and upgrade link is read from the game's own executable — the tables its menus read — and every name from its English text files. Each field was traced to the game code that reads it: sharpness, attack display, rarity colours, Hunting Horn notes and songs, the bowgun reload, recoil and deviation labels, Rapid and Crouching Fire, bow charges, the upgrade trees, armor defense per level and its sphere and zenny costs all follow the game's own code. The equipment icons are the game's own — the equipment box icons in the slots and the detail panel, the class marks in the sidebar — in its own rarity colours.

Not in this version: relic weapons and armor (they are rolled at random), talismans and the coating bottle icons.

Settings

Storage
Save in this browser Keeps your collection here so it loads automatically. Use Save to a file for backups or another device.
Interaction
Click to mark owned and increase level Clicking an open piece again marks it owned, then (armor) raises its level each click.
Ctrl + Click to remove Un-owns a piece of equipment.
Alt + Click to fully upgrade Marks a piece owned at its max level in one click.
Shift + Click to add to checklist Marks a piece as something you plan to build.
Spend materials when you build Marking a checklist piece owned takes what it cost out of the counts you have recorded.
Skills
I use the Awaken skill Elements that need Awaken show plainly and count for the Element filter. Off, they show the game's way, in parentheses, and are left out of it.
